THE CONCEPT

A crowdsourced, altruistic map of open and shared WiFi networks. Every point was published voluntarily by a member of the community so that no one is left without a connection, anywhere in the world.

HOW TO USE IT

Zoom into your area or use "Locate Me". Tap any marker to reveal the network name and password. Only connect to networks you have permission to use.
